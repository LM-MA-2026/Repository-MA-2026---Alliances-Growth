Partner Relatedness and Firm Growth: The Role of Industry Distance and Governance in Strategic Alliances

Master thesis Repository - Lara Mazzarelli

Each part below is a full, rendered analysis report built with Quarto from the R code in this repository.

This repository holds the analysis code for a master's thesis at the University of St. Gallen (HSG) on how industry (SIC-code) distance between alliance partners relates to focal-firm revenue growth, and how alliance governance form (equity-based vs. non-equity) moderates that relationship.

  1. Part 1: Alliance data (SDC Platinum) Reading and cleaning the raw alliance records.
  2. Part 2: Firm data (Compustat) Reading and cleaning the raw firm-year financials.
  3. Part 3: Linking alliances to firms and industry distance Matching alliance partners to firms and computing industry (SIC) distance categories.
  4. Part 4: Variables, analysis sample and winsorisation Building the regression-ready sample and the dependent/control variables.
  5. Part 5: Descriptive statistics Summary statistics and descriptive figures.
  6. Part 6: Regressions Main hypothesis tests (H1, H2) and additional analyses.
  7. Part 7: Robustness checks All robustness checks (R1–R20).
  8. Part 8: Appendix Supplementary appendix tables and figures.

Data & reproducibility

The raw inputs (SDC Platinum alliance data, Compustat firm data, WRDS exchange rates) are licensed data obtained through WRDS/LSEG and are not included in this repository. To rerun the pipeline, obtain your own extracts and point the path variable in 00_config.R at the folder that holds them. Intermediate .rds files are excluded since they are large and fully reproducible from the code.

Requirements: R with data.table, ggplot2, fixest (or equivalent), and Quarto to render the .qmd files.
